How we work together

  1. You tell me about one task

    Which task your team repeats, which tools are involved and what a better result would look like.

  2. We agree the scope and a test

    Before anything is built, we agree what should improve, how we measure it and who owns the workflow.

  3. I build, test and hand over

    A working pilot, tested on real cases and documented so your team can run it. Then we decide whether to expand.

How I keep AI under control

Every agent I build works with written rules. If an action cannot be undone, or affects people outside your team, a person decides it.

The agent

Researches, drafts, prepares data and proposes changes.

A person

Sends, deletes, pays, accepts and deploys.
